# MEMENTO: Teaching LLMs to Manage Their Own Context

## 基本信息
- **arXiv**: [2604.09852](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09852)
- **Authors**: Vasilis Kontonis, Yuchen Zeng, Shivam Garg
- **Conference**: arxiv
- **Year**: 2026
- **Topic**: KV Cache

## Abstract (English)
Reasoning models think in long, unstructured streams with no mechanism for compressing or organizing their own intermediate state. MEMENTO introduces a framework for teaching LLMs to manage their own context during inference, reducing KV cache growth by enabling models to self-organize and compress their reasoning traces.

## Abstract (中文)
推理模型以长而无结构的方式思考，没有压缩或组织自己中间状态的机制。MEMENTO引入一个框架，教导LLM在推理期间管理自己的上下文，通过使模型自组织和压缩推理轨迹来减少KV缓存增长。
