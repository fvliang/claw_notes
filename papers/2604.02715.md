# FluxMoE: Decoupling Expert Residency for High-Performance MoE Serving

## 基本信息
- **arXiv**: [2604.02715](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02715)
- **Authors**: Qingxiu Liu, Cyril Y. He, Hanser Jiang
- **Conference**: arxiv
- **Year**: 2026
- **Topic**: MoE Inference

## Abstract (English)
Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models have become a dominant paradigm for scaling large language models, but their rapidly growing parameter sizes introduce severe challenges for efficient serving. FluxMoE decouples expert residency for high-performance MoE serving, enabling flexible expert management that adapts to workload dynamics.

## Abstract (中文)
混合专家(MoE)模型已成为扩展大语言模型的主导范式，但其快速增长参数量给高效服务带来了严峻挑战。FluxMoE通过解耦专家驻留实现高性能MoE服务，使灵活的专家管理能够适应工作负载动态变化。
