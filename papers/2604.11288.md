# Transactional Attention: Semantic Sponsorship for KV-Cache Retention

## 基本信息
- **arXiv**: [2604.11288](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11288)
- **Authors**: Abhinaba Basu
- **Conference**: arxiv
- **Year**: 2026
- **Topic**: KV Cache

## Abstract (English)
At K=16 tokens (0.4% of a 4K context), every existing KV-cache compression method achieves 0% on credential retrieval. The failure mode is dormant token eviction. Transactional Attention proposes semantic sponsorship for KV-cache retention, preventing critical tokens from being evicted during compression.

## Abstract (中文)
在K=16个token（4K上下文的0.4%）时，每种现有的KV缓存压缩方法在凭证检索上的准确率为0%。失败模式是休眠token被驱逐。Transactional Attention提出KV缓存保留的语义赞助，防止关键token在压缩期间被驱逐。
