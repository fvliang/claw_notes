# Speculative Decoding for Autoregressive Video Generation

- **ArXiv ID:** 2604.17397
- **链接:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17397
- **作者:** Yuezhou Hu, Jintao Zhang
- **会议/来源:** arXiv (2026)
- **主题:** Speculative Decoding, Video Generation Inference

## 摘要 (English)

Autoregressive video diffusion is emerging as a promising paradigm for streaming video synthesis, with step distillation serving as the primary means of accelerating inference. Whether speculative decoding, the dominant acceleration strategy for large language models, can be effectively adapted to autoregressive video generation remains an open question, because video blocks are continuous spatiotemporal tensors with no token-level distribution for exact rejection sampling. We introduce SDVG, which brings speculative decoding to block-based autoregressive video diffusion by replacing token verification with an image-quality router. A 1.3B drafter proposes candidate blocks via four denoising steps; each block is VAE-decoded and scored by ImageReward using worst-frame aggregation—taking the minimum per-frame reward to catch single-frame artifacts that averaging would mask. Blocks scoring above a fixed threshold tau are accepted into the 14B target's KV cache; the rest are regenerated by the target. On 1003 MovieGenVideoBench prompts (832x480), SDVG retains 98.1% of target-only VisionReward quality at a 1.59x speedup, and reaches 2.09x at 95.7% quality retention. The framework is training-free and requires no architectural changes.

## 摴要 (中文)

自回归视频扩散正成为流式视频合成的有前途范式，步蒸馏是加速推理的主要手段。推测解码——大语言模型的主流加速策略——能否有效适配自回归视频生成仍是开放问题，因为视频块是连续时空张量，没有token级分布供精确拒绝采样。我们提出SDVG，通过用图像质量路由器替代token验证，将推测解码引入块级自回归视频扩散。1.3B草稿模型通过四个去噪步骤提出候选块；每个块经VAE解码并用ImageReward评分，采用最差帧聚合。超过阈值tau的块被接受进入14B目标的KV缓存；其余由目标重新生成。在1003个MovieGenVideoBench提示词上，SDVG在1.59x加速下保持98.1%的目标质量，在95.7%质量保持下达到2.09x加速。框架无需训练，无需架构更改。

## 引言要点

- 核心挑战：视频块是连续时空张量，无法用传统token级拒绝采样验证
- 用图像质量路由器(ImageReward + worst-frame aggregation)替代token级验证
- 首个将推测解码成功应用于自回归视频生成的框架
- 无需训练或架构修改，可无缝集成到现有管线

## 关键贡献

1. **SDVG框架**：首个将推测解码适配到块级自回归视频扩散的方法
2. **图像质量路由器**：用ImageReward最差帧聚合替代token级拒绝采样
3. **1.59x-2.09x加速**：在14B模型上实现显著推理加速，质量保持>95%
