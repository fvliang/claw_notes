# Hive: A Multi-Agent Infrastructure for Algorithm- and Task-Level Scaling

- **ArXiv ID:** 2604.17353
- **链接:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17353
- **作者:** Zizhang Luo, Yuhao Luo, Youwei Xiao, Yansong Xu, Runlin Guo, Yun Liang
- **会议/来源:** arXiv (2026)
- **主题:** Inference Scheduling, Multi-Agent Inference

## 摘要 (English)

Large language models are increasingly deployed as complex agentic systems that scale with task complexity. While prior work has extensively explored model- and system-level scaling, algorithm- and task-level scaling remain largely unaddressed, constraining the full potential of agentic systems. At the algorithm level, allocating additional inference-time computation can enhance workflow capacity but introduces cross-path redundancy: overlapping computations across multiple reasoning branches. At the task level, complex tasks can be decomposed into subproblems and delegated across multiple agents for improved scalability and parallelism. However, existing infrastructures' scheduling is unaware of the existence of multiple agents, missing opportunities to optimize resource allocation.

We propose Hive, a multi-agent infrastructure that enables algorithm- and task-level scaling. Hive features a description frontend that captures per-agent behavior and supports test-time scaling algorithms. Leveraging this specification, our backend introduces two key mechanisms: Logits Cache that reuses intermediate logits across redundant sampling paths to mitigate cross-path redundancy at the algorithm level, and Agent-Aware Scheduling that efficiently allocates compute and KV-cache resources according to agent contributions at the task level. Experiments show that Logits Cache achieves an average speedup of 1.11×–1.76× for re-sampling, and Agent-Aware Scheduling reduces the hotspot miss rate by 33%–51%.

## 摘要 (中文)

大语言模型越来越多地以复杂的智能体系统形式部署，随任务复杂度进行扩展。虽然先前工作已广泛探索了模型级和系统级扩展，但算法级和任务级扩展仍未得到充分关注，限制了智能体系统的全部潜力。在算法层面，分配额外的推理时计算可以增强工作流能力，但会引入跨路径冗余：多个推理分支间的重叠计算。在任务层面，复杂任务可分解为子问题并委托给多个智能体以提高可扩展性和并行性。然而，现有基础设施的调度未能感知多智能体的存在，错失了优化资源分配的机会。

我们提出Hive，一个支持算法级和任务级扩展的多智能体基础设施。Hive具有描述前端，捕获每个智能体的行为并支持测试时缩放算法。基于此规范，后端引入两个关键机制：Logits Cache跨冗余采样路径复用中间logits以缓解算法级跨路径冗余，以及Agent-Aware Scheduling根据智能体贡献高效分配计算和KV-cache资源。实验表明Logits Cache实现1.11x-1.76x重采样加速，Agent-Aware Scheduling将热点缺失率降低33%-51%。

## 引言要点

- LLM推理从单轮响应引擎转向智能体问题解决者，需要多步规划、工具调用和协调
- 四种扩展范式：Model-Level（模型规模）、System-Level（硬件并行）、Algorithm-Level（推理时额外计算）、Task-Level（多智能体分解）
- 现有系统主要关注模型级和系统级，算法级和任务级缺少运行时支持
- 跨路径冗余：多推理分支共享公共前缀和中间生成，现有引擎将其视为独立请求导致重复计算
- 多智能体调度挑战：异构运行特征（不同输入输出长度、缓存足迹、上下文复用模式）
- 案例研究：硬件验证智能体（Tree-of-Thought + 多智能体协作）

## 关键贡献

1. **Hive描述前端**：捕获每个智能体的行为规范，支持测试时缩放算法
2. **Logits Cache**：跨冗余采样路径复用中间logits，缓解算法级跨路径冗余
3. **Agent-Aware Scheduling**：根据智能体贡献度分配计算和KV-cache资源
