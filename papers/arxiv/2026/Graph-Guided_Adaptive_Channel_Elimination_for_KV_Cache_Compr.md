## Abstract (English)

We introduce GRACE (Graph-guided Adaptive Channel Elimination), a novel framework that reframes KV cache compression as a graph-based optimization problem. GRACE models channels as nodes and their inter-dependencies as edges, enabling structured and informed pruning decisions.
