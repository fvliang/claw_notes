## Abstract (English)

We show that the attention sink phenomenon corresponds to a stable, reachable, and error-controllable fixed point constructed during training. Based on this insight, we propose SinkRouter, a training-free selective routing framework that detects the sink signal and skips computations that would otherwise produce near-zero output.
