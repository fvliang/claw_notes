# GRASPrune: Global Gating for Budgeted Structured Pruning of Large Language Models

## 基本信息
- **arXiv**: [2604.19398](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19398)
- **Authors**: Ziyang Wang, Jiangfeng Xiao, Chuan Xiao, Ruoxiang Li, Rui Mao, Jianbin Qin
- **Conference**: arxiv
- **Year**: 2026
- **Topic**: LLM Pruning/Serving

## Abstract (English)
Large language models (LLMs) are expensive to serve because model parameters, attention computation, and KV caches impose substantial memory and latency costs. GRASPrune presents a structured pruning framework applied after pretraining that jointly prunes FFN channels and KV head groups under a single global budget. Instead of learning importance scores separately, it uses global gating to determine pruning decisions across the entire model.

## Abstract (中文)
大语言模型服务成本高昂，因为模型参数、注意力计算和KV缓存带来巨大的内存和延迟开销。GRASPrune提出一种后预训练结构化剪枝框架，在单一全局预算下联合剪枝FFN通道和KV头组。它不是分别学习重要性分数，而是使用全局门控来确定整个模型的剪枝决策。
