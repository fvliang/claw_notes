# ReaLB: Real-Time Load Balancing for Multimodal MoE Inference

## 基本信息
- **arXiv**: [2604.19503](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19503)
- **Authors**: Yingping Wang, Yi Wu, Xiangyu Wu
- **Conference**: arxiv
- **Year**: 2026
- **Topic**: MoE Inference

## Abstract (English)
Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures are widely used in modern large language models and multimodal models. However, inference efficiency is often limited by load imbalance across experts. ReaLB proposes real-time load balancing for multimodal MoE inference, dynamically adjusting expert allocation to optimize throughput and reduce latency.

## Abstract (中文)
混合专家(MoE)架构广泛用于现代大语言模型和多模态模型。然而，推理效率常常受限于专家间的负载不平衡。ReaLB提出多模态MoE推理的实时负载均衡，动态调整专家分配以优化吞吐量并降低延迟。
