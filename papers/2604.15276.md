# HadAgent: Harness-Aware Decentralized Agentic AI Serving with Proof-of-Inference Blockchain Consensus

## 基本信息
- **arXiv**: [2604.15276](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15276)
- **Authors**: Landy Jimenez, Mariah Weatherspoon, Bingyu Shen, Yi Sheng, Jianming Liu, Boyang Li
- **Conference**: arxiv
- **Year**: 2026
- **Topic**: LLM Serving

## Abstract (English)
Proof-of-Work blockchain consensus consumes vast computational resources without producing useful output, while the rapid growth of large language model (LLM) agents has created unprecedented demand for GPU computation. HadAgent presents harness-aware decentralized agentic AI serving with proof-of-inference blockchain consensus, combining LLM agent serving with blockchain verification.

## Abstract (中文)
工作量证明区块链共识消耗大量计算资源却不产生有用输出，而大语言模型代理的快速增长创造了前所未有的GPU计算需求。HadAgent提出具有推理证明区块链共识的Harness感知去中心化代理AI服务，将LLM代理服务与区块链验证相结合。
