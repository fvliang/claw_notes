# Graph-Guided Adaptive Channel Elimination for KV Cache Compression

## 基本信息
- **arXiv**: [2604.16983](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16983)
- **Authors**: Enwei Tong, Yao Zhu, Yuanchao Bai
- **Conference**: arxiv
- **Year**: 2026
- **Topic**: KV Cache

## Abstract (English)
Large Language Models have revolutionized natural language processing, achieving unprecedented success across a vast range of tasks. However, their practical deployment is hindered by the significant memory overhead of KV caches during inference. GRACE proposes graph-guided adaptive channel elimination for KV cache compression, using graph-based optimization to determine which channels to eliminate adaptively.
