# Unlocking the Edge Deployment and On-Device Acceleration of Multi-LoRA Enabled One-for-All Foundational LLM

## 基本信息
- **arXiv**: [2604.18655](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18655)
- **Authors**: Sravanth Kodavanti, Sowmya Vajrala, Srinivas Miriyala
- **Conference**: arxiv
- **Year**: 2026
- **Topic**: Edge LLM Serving

## Abstract (English)
Deploying large language models (LLMs) on smartphones poses significant engineering challenges due to stringent constraints on memory, latency, and runtime. This paper explores edge deployment and on-device acceleration of multi-LoRA enabled foundational LLMs, proposing techniques for efficient multi-LoRA serving on resource-constrained edge devices.

## Abstract (中文)
由于内存、延迟和运行时间的严格约束，在智能手机上部署大语言模型面临重大工程挑战。本文探索多LoRA启用的基础LLM的边缘部署和设备端加速，提出在资源受限的边缘设备上实现高效多LoRA服务的技术。
