## 摘要 (Abstract)

DeFT introduces Flash Tree-attention for efficient tree-structured speculative decoding, optimizing the attention computation over tree-shaped token sequences to accelerate LLM inference.

## 摘要 (中文)

DeFT为高效的树形推测解码引入了Flash Tree-attention,优化了树形token序列上的注意力计算,以加速LLM推理。

## 引言 (Introduction)

树形推测解码(Tree-based Speculative Decoding)通过并行生成多个候选token来加速推理,但树状结构的注意力计算复杂度较高。DeFT提出了Flash Tree-attention,利用CUDA内核融合技术高效地处理树形注意力模式。
