# DuetServe: Harmonizing Prefill and Decode for LLM Serving via Adaptive GPU Multiplexing

## 论文信息

- **原文链接**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.02781
- **作者**: Lei Gao, Chaoyi Jiang, Hossein Entezari Zarch, Daniel Wong, Murali Annavaram
- **年份**: 2025
- **来源**: arxiv

## 摘要 (Abstract)

Modern LLM serving systems struggle to balance prefill and decode workloads. DuetServe introduces adaptive GPU multiplexing to dynamically allocate resources between prefill and decode stages, improving overall system throughput and reducing latency.

## 摘要 (中文)

现代LLM服务系统难以平衡预填充和解码工作负载。DuetServe引入自适应GPU多路复用来动态分配预填充和解码阶段之间的资源，提高整体系统吞吐量并降低延迟。
