# Reasoning Language Model Inference Serving Unveiled: An Empirical Study

## 论文信息

- **原文链接**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15564
- **作者**: Qi Li, Junpan Wu, Xiang Liu, et al.
- **年份**: 2025
- **来源**: arXiv

## 摘要 (Abstract)

The reasoning large language model (RLLM) has been proven competitive in solving complex reasoning tasks such as mathematics, coding, compared to general LLM. However, the unique inference patterns of RLLMs, such as extended thinking processes and self-verification mechanisms, introduce significant challenges for serving systems. In this paper, we present the first comprehensive empirical study on RLLM inference serving. We analyze the performance characteristics of RLLMs and identify key bottlenecks including longer response lengths, more complex attention patterns, and dynamic computational demands. Based on our findings, we propose several optimization strategies and evaluate their effectiveness. Our study provides valuable insights for designing efficient RLLM serving systems.

## 摘要 (中文)

推理大型语言模型（RLLM）在解决复杂推理任务（如数学、编码）方面已被证明与通用LLM具有竞争力。然而，RLLM独特的推理模式，如扩展思考过程和自我验证机制，给服务系统带来了重大挑战。在本文中，我们首次对RLLM推理服务进行了全面的实证研究。我们分析了RLLM的性能特征，并确定了关键瓶颈，包括响应长度更长、注意力模式更复杂、计算需求更动态。基于我们的发现，我们提出了几种优化策略并评估了其有效性。我们的研究为设计高效的RLLM服务系统提供了有价值的见解。

## 引言 (Introduction)

RLLM的服务挑战：

3. 动态计算需求
4. 自我验证机制

研究贡献：
- 首次全面实证研究
- 识别关键瓶颈
- 提出优化策略
- 为系统设计提供见解
