# Spiffy: Multiplying Diffusion LLM Acceleration via Lossless Speculative Decoding

## 论文信息

- **原文链接**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510..18115
- **作者**: Sudhanshu Agrawal, Risheek Garrepalli, Raghavv Goel, Mingu Lee, Christopher Lott, Fatih Porikli
- **年份**: 2025
- **来源**: arxiv

## 摘要 (Abstract)

Diffusion-based LLMs offer an alternative to autoregressive generation. Spiffy applies speculative decoding techniques to diffusion models, achieving lossless acceleration of the generation process.

## 摘要 (中文)

基于扩散的LLM为自回归生成提供了一种替代方案。Spiffy将投机解码技术应用于扩散模型，实现了生成过程的无损加速。
