# LLaMCAT: Optimizing Large Language Model Inference with Cache Arbitration and Throttling

## 论文信息

- **原文链接**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13591
- **作者**: Zhongchun Zhou, Chengtao Lai, Wei Zhang
- **年份**: 2025
- **来源**: arxiv

## 摘要 (Abstract)

Large Language Models (LLMs) have achieved unprecedented success but their substantial memory requirements pose significant challenges. LLaMCAT proposes a cache arbitration and throttling mechanism to optimize memory usage and improve inference throughput.

## 摘要 (中文)

大型语言模型(LLM)取得了前所未有的成功，但其大量内存需求带来了重大挑战。LLaMCAT提出了缓存仲裁和节流机制来优化内存使用并提高推理吞吐量。
