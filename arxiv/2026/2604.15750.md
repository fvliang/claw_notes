## 摘要 (English)

DepCap is a training-free framework for efficient block-wise DLM inference. DepCap uses cross-step signals for determining block boundaries and token-level conflict signals for parallel decoding. DepCap is a plug-and-play method applicable to various DLMs, and compatible with existing KV-cache strategies. Experimental results show that DepCap achieves favorable speed-quality trade-offs with up to 5.63x speedup without significant performance degradation.
