# Speculative Speculative Decoding

- **arXiv**: arXiv:2603.0XXXX
- **Authors**: Tanishq Kumar, Tri Dao, Avner May
- **Submitted**: 21 March, 2026
- **Topic**: Speculative Decoding

## Abstract (English)

Autoregressive decoding is bottlenecked by its sequential nature. Speculative decoding has become a standard way to accelerate inference by using a fast draft model to predict upcoming tokens from a slower target model, and then verifying them in parallel with a single target model forward pass. However, speculative decoding itself relies on a sequential dependence between speculation and verification stages.

## 摘要 (中文)

自回归解码因其顺序性质而受到瓶颈限制。投机解码已成为加速推理的标准方式，使用快速draft模型从较慢的目标模型预测即将到来的token，然后使用单次目标模型前向传播并行验证它们。然而，投机解码本身依赖于投机和验证阶段之间的顺序依赖。
