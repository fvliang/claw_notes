## 摘要 (English)

NCCL EP provides unified ncclEpDispatch and ncclEpCombine primitives, supporting Low-Latency (LL) mode for inference decoding and High-Throughput (HT) mode for training and inference prefill. LL targets small batch sizes using direct all-to-all RDMA+NVLink mesh connectivity. HT targets large batches using hierarchical communication. Evaluated on H100-based cluster with vLLM integration.

## 摘要 (中文)

NCCL EP提供统一的ncclEpDispatch和ncclEpCombine原语，支持推理解码的低延迟(LL)模式和训练/prefill的高吞吐量(HT)模式。LL模式针对小批量使用RDMA+NVLink，HT模式针对大批量使用层级通信。已集成vLLM，在H100集群上评估。
