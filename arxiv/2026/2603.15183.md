## 摘要 (English)

Multi-agent LLM orchestration incurs synchronization costs scaling as O(n x S x |D|). This work maps synchronization cost explosion onto the cache coherence problem and adapts MESI-protocol invalidation. Token Coherence Theorem: lazy invalidation attenuates cost by at least S/(n + W(d_i)). Simulation yields token savings of 95.0% at V=0.05. Code at github.com/hipvlady/agent-coherence.

## 摘要 (中文)

多智能体LLM编排产生O(n×S×|D|)同步开销。将同步开销爆炸映射到缓存一致性问题，适配MESI协议无效化。Token一致性定理：惰性无效化至少衰减S/(n+W)开销。模拟显示token节省95%。代码在github.com/hipvlady/agent-coherence。
