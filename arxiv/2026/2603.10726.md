## 摘要 (English)

CacheSolidarity secures multi-tenant LLM serving systems against APC side channels without sacrificing performance. APC works by reusing previously computed states for shared prefixes. While APC improves throughput, it introduces timing side channels. CacheSolidarity monitors cache reuse across users, flags suspicious sharing, and selectively isolates prefixes. Evaluation shows up to 70% higher cache reuse and 30% lower inference latency compared to existing defenses.

## 摘要 (中文)

CacheSolidarity在不牺牲性能的情况下保护多租户LLM服务系统免受APC侧信道攻击。监控跨用户缓存复用，标记可疑共享，选择性隔离前缀。与现有防御相比缓存复用提高70%，推理延迟降低30%。
