## 摘要 (Abstract)

Large Language Models (LLMs) have achieved remarkable success across diverse applications, yet their deployment remains challenging due to substantial computational costs, memory requirements, and energy consumption. ## 引言 (Introduction)

大语言模型的部署面临着多重挑战：
- 计算成本高
- 内存需求大
- 能源消耗高

本文提出自适应效率优化框架AE-LLM，根据不同工作负载动态选择最优的效率优化技术。
