# NOSA: Native and Offloadable Sparse Attention

## 论文信息

- **原文链接**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02549
- **作者**: Yuxiang Huang, Pengjie Wang, Jicheng Han, Weilin Zhao, Zhou Su, Ao Sun, Hongya Lyu, Hengyu Zhao, Yudong Wang, Chaojun Xiao, Xu Han, Zhiyuan Liu
- **年份**: 2026
- **来源**: arxiv

## 摘要 (Abstract)

Decoding throughput improvements from larger batch sizes are limited by the quadratic complexity of attention. NOSA proposes a native and offloadable sparse attention mechanism that reduces computational overhead while maintaining model quality.

## 摘要 (中文)

更大批量的解码吞吐量改进受到注意力二次复杂度的限制。NOSA提出了一种原生可卸载的稀疏注意力机制，在保持模型质量的同时降低计算开销。
