## 摘要 (Abstract)

Agentic workflows are composed of sequences of interdependent Large Language Model (LLM) calls, and they have become a dominant workload in modern AI systems. This paper examines LLM serving from a data systems perspective, focusing on the unique challenges posed by agentic workflows.

智能体工作负载由相互依赖的LLM调用序列组成，已成为现代AI系统中的主导工作负载。本文从数据系统角度研究LLM服务，重点关注智能体工作负载带来的独特挑战。
