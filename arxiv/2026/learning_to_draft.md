# Learning to Draft: Adaptive Speculative Decoding with Reinforcement Learning

- **arXiv**: arXiv:2603.0XXXX
- **Authors**: Jiebin Zhang, Zhenghan Yu, Liang Wang, Nan Yang, Eugene J. Yu, Zheng Li, Yifan Song, Dawei Zhu, Xingxing Zhang, Furu Wei, Sujian Li
- **Submitted**: 12 March, 2026
- **Topic**: Speculative Decoding

## Abstract (English)

Speculative decoding accelerates large language model (LLM) inference by using a small draft model to generate candidate tokens for a larger target model to verify. The efficacy of this technique hinges on the trade-off between the time spent on drafting and the quality of draft tokens.

## 摘要 (中文)

投机解码通过使用小型draft模型为更大的目标模型生成候选token来加速大型语言模型(LLM)推理。该技术的效能取决于draft所花费的时间与draft token质量之间的权衡。
