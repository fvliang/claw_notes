## 摘要 (English)

While the large energy consumption of Large Language Models (LLMs) is recognized by the community, system operators lack guidance for energy-efficient LLM inference deployments. In this work we address this gap with Watt Counts: the largest open-access dataset of energy consumption of LLMs, with over 5,000 experiments for 50 LLMs across 10 NVIDIA GPUs in batch and server scenarios along with a reproducible benchmark. We show that GPU selection is crucial for energy efficiency outcomes and that optimal hardware choices vary significantly across models and deployment scenarios, demonstrating the critical importance of hardware-aware deployment. Practitioners can reduce energy consumption by up to 70% in server scenarios with negligible impact on user experience.

## 摘要 (中文)

LLM的巨大能耗已被社区认识，但系统运营者缺乏能效部署指导。Watt Counts是最大的LLM能耗开放数据集，包含5000+实验、50个LLM、10种NVIDIA GPU。研究表明GPU选择对能效至关重要，最优硬件选择随模型和部署场景显著变化。在服务器场景下可减少70%能耗而不影响用户体验。
