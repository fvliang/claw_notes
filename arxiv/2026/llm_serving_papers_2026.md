# A Pipelined Collaborative Speculative Decoding Framework for Efficient Edge-Cloud LLM Inference

## 基本信息

- **标题**: A Pipelined Collaborative Speculative Decoding Framework for Efficient Edge-Cloud LLM Inference
- **作者**: Yida Zhang, Zhiyong Gao, Shuaibing Yue, Jie Li, Rui Wang
- **arXiv**: [2603.XXXXX](https://arxiv.org/abs/)
- **发布时间**: 2026年3月19日

## 摘要 (Abstract)

Recent advancements and widespread adoption of Large Language Models (LLMs) in both industry and academia have catalyzed significant demand for efficient LLM inference systems. This paper presents a pipelined collaborative speculative decoding framework that leverages edge and cloud resources to accelerate LLM inference while maintaining quality.

## 摘要 (中文)
