## 摘要 (English)

Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) enable powerful cross-modal reasoning capabilities but impose substantial computational and latency burdens, posing critical challenges for deployment on resource-constrained edge devices. We propose MSAO, an adaptive modality sparsity-aware offloading framework with edge-cloud collaboration for efficient MLLM Inference. First, a lightweight heterogeneous modality-aware via fine-grained sparsity module performs spatial-temporal-modal joint analysis to compute the Modality Activation Sparsity (MAS) metric. Second, an adaptive speculative edge-cloud collaborative offloading mechanism dynamically schedules workloads between edge and cloud based on derived MAS scores and real-time system states. Experiments demonstrate that MSAO achieves a 30% reduction in end-to-end latency and 30%-65% decrease in resource overhead, while delivering a throughput improvement of 1.5x to 2.3x.

## 摘要 (中文)

多模态LLM实现强大的跨模态推理但带来巨大的计算和延迟负担。提出MSAO，自适应模态稀疏感知卸载框架，通过边云协作实现高效多模态LLM推理。通过时空模态联合分析计算模态激活稀疏度指标，动态调度边云负载。端到端延迟降低30%，资源开销降低30%-65%，吞吐量提升1.5-2.3倍。
