## 摘要 (English)

HeteroServe proposes phase-aware runtime with modality-level partitioning and cross-tier scheduling for multimodal LLM inference. Modality boundary minimizes cross-device transfer. Heterogeneous deployment is cost-optimal under phase-separable workloads (predicts 31.4% savings; observed 40.6%). Under fixed budget, a heterogeneous cluster ($38k) improves Tokens/$ by 37%.

## 摘要 (中文)

HeteroServe提出模态级分区和跨层调度用于多模态LLM推理。模态边界最小化跨设备传输。异构部署在相位可分负载下成本最优（预测31.4%节省，实测40.6%）。固定预算下异构集群提升Tokens/$ 37%。
