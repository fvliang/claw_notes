## 摘要 (English)

RAG systems enhance LLM performance by incorporating supplementary retrieved documents, enabling more accurate and context-aware responses. However, integrating these external documents often results in very long input sequences, which significantly increases computation costs during the prefill stage. KV-cache reuse offers a promising solution by storing previously computed KV states for shared input prefixes. PCR introduces three key techniques: (1) a prefix-tree caching structure with a look-ahead LRU replacement policy; (2) layer-wise overlapping that pipelines KV-cache loading and GPU computation across CUDA streams; and (3) queue-based prefetching that proactively loads relevant KV caches from SSD into DRAM. PCR achieves up to a 2.47x speedup in terms of average TTFT.

## 摘要 (中文)

RAG系统通过引入检索文档增强LLM性能，但导致很长的输入序列，显著增加prefill计算成本。KV缓存复用通过存储共享前缀的KV状态避免冗余计算，但受限于低缓存命中率、CPU-GPU数据传输开销和慢SSD I/O。PCR引入三种关键技术：前缀树缓存结构加前瞻LRU替换策略、层级重叠流水线化KV缓存加载和GPU计算、队列预取主动从SSD加载KV缓存。平均TTFT加速2.47倍。
