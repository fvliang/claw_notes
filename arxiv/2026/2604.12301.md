## 摘要 (English)

We present a systematic measurement study of seven tactics for reducing cloud LLM token usage when a small local model can act as a triage layer in front of a frontier cloud model. Local routing combined with prompt compression achieves 45-79% cloud token savings on edit-heavy and explanation-heavy workloads.

系统测量研究七种降低云LLM token使用量的策略，当小本地模型可作为前沿云模型的前端分流层。本地路由加提示压缩在编辑密集和解释密集工作负载上实现45-79%云token节省。
