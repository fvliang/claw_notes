# Balancing Coverage and Draft Latency in Vocabulary Trimming for Faster Speculative Decoding

- **arXiv**: arXiv:2603.0XXXX
- **Authors**: Ofir Ben Shoham
- **Submitted**: 5 March, 2026
- **Topic**: Speculative Decoding

## Abstract (English)

Speculative decoding accelerates inference for Large Language Models by using a lightweight draft model to propose candidate tokens that are verified in parallel by a larger target model. Prior work shows that the draft model often dominates speculative decoding latency, since it generates tokens sequentially and incurs high cost from its language modeling head as vocabulary size grows.

## 摘要 (中文)

投机解码通过使用轻量级draft模型提出候选token来加速大型语言模型的推理，这些token由更大的目标模型并行验证。之前的工作表明，draft模型通常主导投机解码延迟，因为它顺序生成token，并且随着词汇量增长，其语言建模头会产生很高的成本。
