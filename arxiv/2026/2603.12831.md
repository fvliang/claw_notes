## 摘要 (English)

OmniServe proposes Attention Piggybacking mechanism that offloads Attention computation of best-effort services to CPUs on the fly, facilitating asynchronous communication between CPU and GPU streams. OmniServe improves SLO attainment rate for latency-sensitive services by up to 1.48x while enhancing best-effort serving throughput by up to 9.85x.

## 摘要 (中文)

OmniServe提出注意力搭载机制，将BE服务的注意力计算卸载到CPU，实现CPU-GPU异步通信。SLO达成率提高1.48倍，BE吞吐量提高9.85倍。
