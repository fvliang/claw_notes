## 摘要 (English)

Speculative decoding enables collaborative LLM inference across cloud and edge by separating lightweight token drafting from heavyweight verification. Practical deployment requires navigating a large configuration space spanning draft model, verification model, parallelism, and placement. ConfigSpec introduces profiling-based configuration selection for optimal deployment.

## 摘要 (中文)

投机解码通过将轻量级token草拟与重量级验证分离，实现云边协作LLM推理。实际部署需在草拟模型、验证模型、并行度和放置等大量配置空间中导航。ConfigSpec提出基于性能分析的配置选择方法。
