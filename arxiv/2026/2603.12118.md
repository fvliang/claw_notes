## 摘要 (English)

Cornserve provides a flexible task abstraction for expressing Any-to-Any model computation graphs, enabling component disaggregation and independent scaling. The distributed runtime dispatches compute via an efficient record-and-replay execution model. Cornserve delivers up to 3.81x higher throughput and 5.79x lower tail latency. Open-source at github.com/cornserve-ai/cornserve.
