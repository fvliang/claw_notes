## 摘要 (English)

Speculative decoding (SD) accelerates language model inference by drafting tokens from a cheap proposal model and verifying them against an expensive target model via rejection sampling. Because rejection truncates the draft block at the first error, throughput degrades when draft and target diverge. We introduce sequential Monte Carlo speculative decoding (SMC-SD), which replaces token-level rejection with importance-weighted resampling over a population of draft particles. SMC-SD is a principled approximate inference scheme that trades exactness for additional speed, while preserving theoretical bounds on its per-step approximation error. Empirically, SMC-SD achieves 2.36x speed-up over speculative decoding and a 5.2x speed-up over autoregressive decoding, while remaining within 3% of the target model's accuracy.
