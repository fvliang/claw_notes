# Speculative Decoding Scaling Laws (SDSL): Throughput Optimization Made Simple

## 摘要 (Abstract)

### English
We present Speculative Decoding Scaling Laws (SDSL), a systematic framework for optimizing speculative decoding throughput. Unlike prior work that focuses on individual components, SDSL provides a holistic analysis of how different parameters (draft model size, number of speculation tokens, verification efficiency) affect end-to-end throughput. We derive analytical formulas that predict throughput scaling and validate them across diverse workloads. Our framework enables practitioners to make informed decisions about speculative decoding configurations without extensive experimentation.

### 中文
我们提出了投机解码缩放定律（SDSL），这是一个优化投机解码吞吐量的系统性框架。与之前专注于单个组件的工作不同，SDSL提供了关于不同参数（起草模型大小、投机token数量、验证效率）如何影响端到端吞吐量的整体分析。我们推导了预测吞吐量缩放的解析公式，并在各种工作负载上验证了它们。我们的框架使从业者能够在没有大量实验的情况下对投机解码配置做出明智的决策。
