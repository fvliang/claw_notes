## 摘要 (English)

S-HPLB proposes sparsity-aware head-parallel load balance for efficient LLM attention serving. Attention heads exhibit heterogeneous-yet-stable sparsity elasticities, motivating head-adaptive sparsity budgets. S-HPLB can achieve a 2.88x improvement in average attention computation latency without quality degradation.

## 摘要 (中文)

S-HPLB提出稀疏感知的头并行负载均衡用于高效LLM注意力服务。注意力头展现异构但稳定的稀疏弹性。平均注意力计算延迟改善2.88倍，无质量退化。
