# CHESS: Context-aware Hierarchical Efficient Semantic Selection for Long-Context LLM Inference

## 论文信息

- **原文链接**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.02938
- **作者**: Chao Fei, Guozhong Li, Chenxi Liu, Panos Kalnis
- **年份**: 2026
- **来源**: arxiv

## 摘要 (Abstract)

Long-context LLMs demand accurate and efficient context selection mechanisms. We propose CHESS, a hierarchical semantic selection framework that efficiently identifies the most relevant context segments for each query. Our approach reduces computational complexity while maintaining model quality.

## 摘要 (中文)

长上下文LLM需要准确和高效的上下文选择机制。我们提出了CHESS，一个分层语义选择框架，可以有效地为每个查询识别最相关的上下文片段。我们的方法在保持模型质量的同时降低了计算复杂度。
