# RelayCaching: Accelerating LLM Collaboration via Decoding KV Cache Reuse

## 论文信息

- **作者**: Yingsheng Geng, Yuchong Gao, Weihong Wu, Guyue Liu, Jiang Liu
- **arXiv**: [2603.13289](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13289)
- **提交日期**: 2026年2月27日
- **领域**: Machine Learning (cs.LG); Artificial Intelligence (cs.AI)

## 摘要 (Abstract)

The increasing complexity of AI tasks has shifted the paradigm from monolithic models toward multi-agent large language model (LLM) systems. However, these collaborative architectures introduce a critical bottleneck: redundant prefill computation for shared content generated by previous agents, which significantly increases KV cache memory usage and time-to-first-token (TTFT). While various KV cache methods have been proposed to mitigate prefill redundancy, they either fail to maintain accuracy on agent-generated outputs or exhibit low reuse rates due to rigid constraints. We present RelayCaching, a training-free inference method that directly reuses decoding phase KV caches from previous agents in subsequent prefill phases. Our key insight is that KV caches for identical content are highly consistent across phases, while prefix-induced deviations are sparse and localized within a limited range of layers and token positions. By selectively recomputing KV caches at these positions, RelayCaching preserves model accuracy with minimal overhead, yielding a superior accuracy-efficiency trade-off over existing methods. Experiments on diverse collaborative LLM tasks spanning mathematical reasoning, general knowledge, and code generation demonstrate that RelayCaching achieves over 80% KV cache reuse, reduces TTFT by up to 4.7× compared to the standard pipeline, all with negligible accuracy degradation.

## 摘要 (中文)

AI任务复杂性的增加已使范式从单一模型转向多代理大语言模型(LLM)系统。然而，这些协作架构引入了一个关键瓶颈：先前代理生成的共享内容的冗余预填充计算，这显著增加了KV缓存内存使用和首token时间(TTFT)。虽然已提出各种KV缓存方法来减轻预填充冗余，但它们要么无法在代理生成的输出上保持准确度，要么由于刚性约束而表现出较低的复用率。我们提出了RelayCaching，一种无训练推理方法，可直接在后续预填充阶段重用先前代理的解码阶段KV缓存。我们的关键见解是，相同内容的KV缓存在各阶段高度一致，而前缀诱导的偏差稀疏且局限于有限的层和token位置范围内。通过在这些位置有选择性地重新计算KV缓存，RelayCaching以最小开销保持模型准确度，实现了优于现有方法的准确度-效率权衡。在跨越数学推理、常识和代码生成的多样化协作LLM任务上的实验表明，RelayCaching实现了超过80%的KV缓存复用，与标准管道相比将TTFT降低高达4.7倍，同时准确度损失可忽略不计。

## 引言 (Introduction)

多代理LLM系统是当前AI的重要发展方向，但存在计算冗余问题。RelayCaching通过重用解码阶段的KV缓存来解决这一问题，无需训练即可实现高效的代理协作。
