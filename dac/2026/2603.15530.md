## 摘要 (English)

DUET introduces a disaggregated accelerator that assigns prefill and decode phases to specialized packages. The Prefill package utilizes systolic array chiplets with off-package memory. The Decode package utilizes vector-unit arrays with high-bandwidth in-package memory. Evaluations show DUET achieves 4x faster TTFT, 1.4x higher throughput, and 1.5x lower TPOT over the B200 GPU.

## 摘要 (中文)

DUET引入解耦加速器，将prefill和decode阶段分配到专用包。Prefill包使用脉动阵列芯片，Decode包使用向量单元阵列和高带宽封装内存。比B200 GPU快4倍TTFT、1.4倍吞吐量、1.5倍更低TPOT。
